How K-Means Works

Step 1 — Initialize: Choose K initial centroids.

Step 2 — Assignment: Calculate the distance between every point and every centroid. Assign each point to the nearest centroid.

Step 3 — Update: Calculate the mean of all points belonging to each cluster.

Step 4 — Repeat: The newly calculated means become the new centroids.

Step 5 — Convergence: Stop when the centroids no longer change.
