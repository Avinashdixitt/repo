About Me

I am a Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Indore under the supervision of Prof. Surya Prakash. My research focuses on Livestock Biometrics, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Pattern Recognition. Prior to joining IIT Indore, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering and worked as a Software Engineer at Round The Clock Technologies. I have also gained industrial experience through internships in Machine Learning, Internet of Things (IoT), and Robotics.
