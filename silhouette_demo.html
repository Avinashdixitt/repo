Cluster Validation: Silhouette Intuition
For a point $i$:
  • $a(i)$ = average distance from $i$ to points in its own cluster.
  • $b(i)$ = smallest average distance from $i$ to points in another cluster.
Silhouette:
$$s(i)= \frac{b(i)-a(i)} {\max(a(i),b(i))}$$
$$-1\leq s(i)\leq1$$
● near 1: well matched to its own cluster
● near 0: boundary / overlapping point
● negative: possibly assigned to the wrong cluster
